Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In short: Lulo Smaç! needs no account and never connects to the internet. There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools. Everything in the game stays on your device; no data ever reaches us.

What data does the game collect?

None. Your child's progress, coins, jerseys, costumes and settings are stored only on the device and are never sent to us. If the app is deleted, they are deleted too. The game never asks for an account, name, email address, location, photos, camera, microphone or contacts.

Internet

The game never connects to the internet and sends no data to any server. The one exception is purchases: when buying, restoring or asking for a refund, the device talks directly to Apple's App Store.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see card or payment details. The game only checks on the device, through Apple's system, which packs were bought. With Family Sharing on, bought packs also unlock on the family's other devices.

Parental gate

Prices, purchases, restoring and refund requests sit behind a multiplication question for a grown-up, in the Parents' Corner (Ebeveyn Köşesi) of the Sports Shop (Spor Dükkânı). With Ask to Buy on in Family Sharing, the request goes to a parent first.

Ads and tracking

There are no ads in the game. We don't track you or your child across apps or websites, and we don't use third-party analytics, ad or chat tools. The game sends no notifications.

Children

Lulo Smaç! is made for children. We don't collect personal data such as names, email addresses, locations or photos from children. There is no chat or contact with other players in the game.

This website

lulosmac.com uses no cookies and no visitor analytics. The game scene and sounds on the home page run in your browser; nothing is saved. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. To deliver the site and protect it from attacks, GitHub may briefly process technical data such as IP addresses. This is based on our legitimate interest in running the site securely.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them within a reasonable time once we are done. Our email is handled by Google Workspace.

International transfers

GitHub, Apple and Google may process data outside Türkiye. These transfers are made with the safeguards required by applicable law.

Your rights

To use your rights under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and, if you are in the European Union, under the GDPR, write to support@lulosmac.com. You may also contact the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

If we update this policy, we change the date at the top of this page. If a feature that collects data is ever added to the game, this page is updated before that feature goes live.
